- Njuškalo direct automated access was already challenged before this task; **no direct automated Njuškalo attempts or challenge evasion** were made. Its evidence here is explicitly search-index-only. Crozilla returned a bot-challenge page through search and was not pursued.
- Index text fetch was empty; normal headless Chrome rendering made selected pages readable. Some navigations initially stalled or showed loading shells; those states were not interpreted as inactive. No ongoing collection, scraper, dependencies or UI/data changes were introduced.
- Oglasnik text fetch showed only a shell; normal browser rendering revealed the inactive marker on the 65 m² listing. This demonstrates why attractive snippets alone are insufficient.
- Independent-agency coverage: directly read the Imperium Laščina detail page, finding internal conflicts and a 2010 renovation; searched Dogma's Trnovčica offer (over budget). - Search also returned aggregation pages from GoHome, Bijelo Jaje, SveHrNekretnine, Cackalo, NadjiDom and Nekretnine.hr. These were discovery aids, not independent confirmation of current vacancy. Search was eventually rate-limited (HTTP 429); no workaround attempted.

- No private seller names, phone numbers or email addresses are reproduced. A neutral, working ID-based Index URL is used for I:7581070 because its original title/slug embeds contact details.
